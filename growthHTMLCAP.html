My Growth in Computer Science

What is the name of the project you are proud of?

How does this work represent an area of growth for you? Cite specific examples.

I think this project represents an area of growth for me because we have been working on app lab and just using ko.org in general throughout the first semester and some of the second semester and I think that it really shows how far I’ve come along since the start of the school year to now because of the more difficult code that is being put into my project. Some specific things that I think really show my growth in this project are the background and design and all my variables that I used to make the game actually run like the score and the Health so it updates each time the player interacts with the obstacles for the goals.

What were the hardest areas for you to master and why?

Some obstacles I faced were getting the instruction screen to pop up and then having it go away when the player presses a button some other things that were difficult we’re making all the functions for everything like the instructions screen or the winning screen or just the function that updates when the player interacts with a Sprite.

How did your level of effort in class or on this specific project directly influence your success?

The level of effort in class and on the specific project directly influence, my success by it just running very smoothly and getting the job done. I think it also influences my success because I really made something that I thought of in my head really come to life and I think that’s very special.

Knowing what I know now, I would probably improve the sprites and how big they are because it is kind of hard to try to avoid the obstacles and get the treats. I would also maybe see if I could add more sprites to make my game more difficult in another way, rather than just speeding up the sprites.
